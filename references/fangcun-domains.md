## Shared selection policy

- Target 10–15 new or updated candidates per domain per run.
- Only Q1, CAS division-1, or domain-recognized flagship journals enter the main pool.
- Recency priority: last 12 months > last 3 years > 3–5 years with strong relevance.
- Deduplicate by DOI, title, Zotero item key, historical drafts, and published records.
- Quality beats volume. Never lower the journal tier merely to hit the candidate target.
